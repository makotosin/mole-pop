MOLE POP! — Playable prototype (English UI)

Open index.html in Chrome, Edge, or Safari.
Tap or click a mole. Hit brown moles (+10) and gold moles (+30). Avoid bomb moles (-10).
The game lasts 30 seconds; the best score is saved locally when available.

Prototype only. YouTube Playables SDK, certification, and real-device validation are not yet completed.
